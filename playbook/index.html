Formations & players

Select a player on the field to rename or remove them. Setting a formation keeps each player's route when the labels match.

Field size

Length is the total shown, and backfield is how much of it sits behind the line of scrimmage. Players stay put but are pulled inside the lines if the field gets smaller.
